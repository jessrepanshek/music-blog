## First impressions

If I were aboard a doomed spaceship hurtling through the universe, this would be the soundtrack. An absolutely beautiful work of art from beginning to end, with pulsing basslines, bubbling synths, and etheral vocals. The juxtaposition of Jared's deep, vampirically-hypnotizing voice with Haley's sultry vocals stands out among a sea of synthwave acts. Their voices dance back and forth across tracks, covering space and time. Full of nostalgic 80s vibes and catchy hooks, I can't help but drum and sing along.
