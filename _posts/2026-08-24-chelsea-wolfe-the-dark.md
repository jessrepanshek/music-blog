## First impressions

I've listened to Chelsea Wolfe on and off for years. I first came across their music when I went to see Youth Code, one of the my favorite industrial acts, open for Chelsea. While I've enjoyed songs here and there, they haven't really stuck with me very much. This album seems a bit different from Chelsea's recent style, and it really scratches an itch I've been searching for. Beautifully haunting vocals and enchanting harmonies, layered over deep acoustic guitar and paino chords. Seethe, the second track, picks up the pace and adds a pulsing bassline that ratchets up the energy. This album immediately grabbed my attention, and before I knew it I was 8 tracks deep. 

This album is a wild contrast from my other favorite work, the Bloodmoon: I album where they partnered up with Converge. These two albums together show quite a range.
