## First impressions

Released in 1997 during what some consider the height of the 90s dance scene, Our Little Secret is the third album (and my second) from the Lords of Acid. This album is full of bangin' beats and distorted synths, deep bass and sultry vocals. At points they pull of a pretty great industrial vibe, adding a dark, sexy edge to their catchy dance rhythms. At times it gets amped up, with tracks like Deep Sexy Space (Chorale) giving almost a happy hardcore/gabber level BPM. And who wouldn't love the final track of the album, Horror Movies, a cheeky remix of an earlier track, Cybersex (Sherzo), replacing the lyrics with classic horror movie tropes.

I was hooked on this album and it quickly fell into my regular rotation. This, as well as VooDoo-U, were perhaps some of the most important albums in ushering my into the dance and electronic music scenes. It's not a perfect album. A couple tracks fall into the straight-up goofy category, which clashes a bit with what I enjoy about the rest of the vibe.
